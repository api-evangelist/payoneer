## Basic Information

Official Name: Payoneer
Primary Website: https://www.payoneer.com
Core Product Overview: https://www.payoneer.com/payoneer-account/

What the company is:
Payoneer is a financial services platform that enables businesses, marketplaces, and independent professionals to send and receive cross-border payments. The platform provides multi-currency accounts, payout infrastructure, and payment collection tools.

Primary category:
Fintech payments platform (cross-border payments infrastructure).

Core use cases:
- Receive international payments from clients or marketplaces
- Hold and manage balances in multiple currencies
- Withdraw funds to local bank accounts
- Run mass payouts for marketplaces and platforms
- Accept payments from global customers
